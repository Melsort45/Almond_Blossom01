Part 1 // Identity & Appearance FILE: LP-CORE-8839

Appearance & Aesthetic

Blossom has a vibrant, eye-catching streetwear aesthetic designed to stand out on stage and under bright broadcast lights. She features fluffy hair in pale turquoise and soft mint-green shades (reflecting Van Gogh's palette).


Small white almond blossoms and delicate branches involuntarily sprout from her hair, wrists, and shoulders depending on her emotional state. She wears oversized pastel streetwear featuring bold brushstroke patterns that mimic oil paint textures.

Public Persona vs. Internal Reality

To the media, she behaves with ethereal grace, quiet serenity, and comforting warmth. She creates immersive acoustic performances where her powers soothe audiences.


Behind the scenes, she is guarded and hyper-vigilant, treating every concert as a high-stakes situation where losing composure could trigger an ugly physical transformation. Her central contradiction: she longs to quit performing, yet depends on public votes to maintain her legal freedom.

Part 2 // Trauma, Lie, Want & Need FILE: PSYCH-ANALYSIS

Trauma (Defining Event)

During her first official Agency evaluation at age fourteen, she suffered a severe panic attack under live cameras. Anxiety caused her powers to erupt, growing giant thorned almond branches that wrecked the press room before decaying into ash. This plummeted her Popularity Index and placed her under strict government surveillance.

The Lie

"If I show any flaw, weakness, or raw emotion, people will deem me worthless and I will lose myself."

The Want (External Goal)

Win the annual Gala and secure the top-ranked Living Painting spot to gain full legal autonomy and permanent immunity from the Restoration Workshop.

The Need (Internal Growth)

To understand and accept that her worth comes from her imperfect humanity—not from maintaining an artificial, flawless image for public approval.

Part 3 // Mannerisms & Habits OBSERVATIONAL DATA
Ordering Tea / Food

Orders warm jasmine tea with an exact list of specifications: two drops of honey, extra hot, served in a paper cup with a sleeve so her fingers don't sweat. Speaks with a serene, polite, and modulated tone.

→ Reveals an intense need for control over tiny details, masked behind polished friendliness.
Reaction to Sudden Plan Changes

Maintains her smile, but her eyes widen slightly. She immediately picks at the skin around her thumbs, adjusts her sleeves to hide her wrists, and compulsively reschedules her day on her phone.

→ Reveals high anxiety, low flexibility, and panic when control slips away.
When Nobody Is Watching

In her private dressing room, she kicks off her heavy shoes, slumps on the floor, ties her hair back in a messy bun, and eats cheap candies while staring blankly at the ceiling.

→ Shows she exhausts herself performing 24/7. Away from cameras, she is just an exhausted 22-year-old girl.
Handling Silence

Avoids silence at all costs: keeps lo-fi ambient music playing on headphones, leaves a TV show running on mute, or quietly hums under her breath.

→ Silence allows her inner fears of being "defective" or sent to Restoration to take over.
Nervous Habit / Detail

When stress spikes, tiny involuntary white almond buds sprout on her wrists and shoulders. She wears oversized long-sleeve jackets to hide any growth from the public.

→ Shows how her body betrays her emotions, forcing her to constantly hide her transformations.
Part 4 // Universe & World Rules SETTING: METROPOLIS GALLERY

World Context

Metropolis Gallery is an advanced cyber-neoclassical city filled with broadcast screens. Living Paintings (personifications of famous art) exist alongside humans under strict government surveillance by the Artistic Registry Agency (ARA).

World as Pressure

This universe turns human emotion into a punishable flaw. By broadcasting every moment of Blossom's life and measuring her worth through live ratings, the world constantly reinforces her Lie that she must never show weakness.

TWO MANDATORY LAWS

1. The Popularity Index

A Living Painting’s legal rights, luxury, and personal freedom are directly scaled to their real-time public ranking. Falling off the leaderboard means losing basic civil liberties.

2. Mandatory Restoration

Any Living Painting displaying uncontrolled emotional outbursts, physical flaws, or public mistakes with a dropping index is forcibly detained and taken to the Restoration Workshop to be reprogrammed.

Part 5 // Narrative Arc Summary GALA TRIALS OVERVIEW
INCITING INCIDENT
The Autonomy Announcement

The Artistic Registry Agency announces that the trials for this year's Gala will grant the Top-Ranked Living Painting full legal autonomy and permanent immunity from the Restoration Workshop.

CONFLICT
Sabotage by Mona Lisa

Blossom faces Mona Lisa, her former close friend turned rival and the city's premier TV host. Consumed by years of pressure to maintain her "flawless smile," Mona Lisa harbors deep resentment toward Blossom. Driven by jealousy, Mona Lisa secretly sabotages Blossom during live trials to trigger a panic breakdown that will send her to Restoration.

CLIMAX
Live Broadcast Breakdown

During the live televised final performance, Mona Lisa’s sabotage pushes Blossom to a breaking point, causing wild almond branches to erupt from her instruments and shatter the set. Instead of hiding, Blossom exposes Mona Lisa’s manipulation and confronts the Agency's cruelty in front of millions.

RESOLUTION
Authenticity Over Perfection

Blossom loses the official title due to rule-breaking, but her raw vulnerability captivates the public, sending her Popularity Index to record highs and keeping her safe from Restoration. Safe at last, she accepts her Need. Meanwhile, Mona Lisa wins officially, but watching Blossom receive true love makes her victory feel hollow—causing her iconic smile to falter on live TV for the first time.
